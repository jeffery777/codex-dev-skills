## Reading and writing cases

The authoritative condition remains in fixed `wf-writing`, not an executable
classifier in the adapter. The following are semantic review cases, not model
observations, an extra gate or a claim of STE certification:

| Case | Expected reading and behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Routine engineering handoff or incidental record | Use minimum writing requirements; no automatic full writing load. |
| A summary document is the requested artifact | Read full writing; its name does not exempt it. |
| Substantive drafting, editing, translation or document review | Read full writing, preserving other applicable contracts. |
| A short change to a step, risk or important conclusion | Treat as substantive and read full writing. |
| Pure spelling/formatting without meaning changes | Minimum can suffice after context check, unless stricter requirements apply. |
| General article | Preserve genre and author voice; do not force procedural fields or invent experiences. |
| Technical or mixed document | Preserve precision, necessary terms and literals; check applicable prerequisites, sequence, expected result and failure treatment. |
| Supported attributed citation, not personally repeated | Check its specific claim and conditions; no blanket experiment-repetition duty. |
| Author's own research or comparison | Require proportionate real evidence, methods and relevant baseline; no borrowed personal measurements. |
| Missing or unsupported source in an existing draft | Preserve the claim and mark it for checking/author confirmation; do not fabricate support or silently change meaning. |
| New draft or conflicting sources | Add no unsupported factual/effect claim; retain applicable versions and conflicts, and do not write unresolved safety content as definite instructions. |
| English technical STE notice | Equivalent meaning may state full conformance is unverified; retain stricter downstream wording/placement. No tool/self-assessment proof of full conformance. |
| Traditional Chinese or translation | Use natural Taiwan wording and consistent terms; preserve bilingual important information and authority, without claiming an official Chinese ASD standard. |

Writing depth changes do not waive engineering, security, authorization, review,
evidence, exact-head or stricter project obligations. Selecting the standalone
writing contract alone does not impose an engineering workflow on a general article.
